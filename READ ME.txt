NEUST GABALDON CAMPUS

First time on a new computer?
1. Double-click "Install Dependencies.cmd" (or check TRANSFER-AND-RUN.txt).
2. Double-click "Start Phone Preview.cmd" to launch the Ionic phone preview.

Or, from lab-app, run: npm start -- --port 8100
Open http://localhost:8100

Pages: Dashboard, School Profile, Courses Offered, Faculty, Contacts, Gallery.
The sidebar stays visible on large screens and opens from the menu button on phones.

Content: src/app/campus.page.ts
Page layout: src/app/campus.page.html
Page styles: src/app/campus.page.scss
Navigation: src/app/app.component.ts
Images: src/assets/campus
